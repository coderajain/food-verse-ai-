Everything You Need for Food AI

Four powerful AI-driven features built on Google Gemini's multimodal capabilities.

📸
Food Recognition
Upload any food photo → Gemini Vision identifies the dish, provides recipes, nutritional estimates, and cultural history with 4 analysis modes.
💬
AI Food Chat
Conversational AI agent with 6 chef personas — World Expert, Professional Chef, Nutrition Coach, Vegan Specialist, Asian Expert & Italian Maestro.
🥗
Nutrition Analyzer
Get detailed calorie, macro & micronutrient breakdowns from food images or text descriptions, with interactive charts and daily goal tracking.
📋
Recipe Generator
Generate personalized recipes from available ingredients, cuisine preferences, food photos, or get a full 7-day meal plan with shopping list.

Deploy Your Own Demo

Get a live public URL in under 5 minutes — completely free.

1

Get Your Free Gemini API Key

Go to aistudio.google.com → Click "Create API Key" → Copy the key

2

Go to Streamlit Community Cloud

Visit share.streamlit.io → Sign in with GitHub → Click "New app"

3

Connect This Repository

Repository: coderajain/food-verse-ai- · Branch: main · File: app.py

4

Add API Key as Secret

Click "Advanced settings" → Secrets → paste: GEMINI_API_KEY = "your_key_here"

5

🚀 Deploy!

Click Deploy — your live URL will be ready in ~2 minutes. Share it on SkillWallet!
